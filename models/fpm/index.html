FPM surface probing

Measured data from the FPM_control scripts. The machine is driven over serial with G-code (Marlin/Klipper). At each probe point it steps down until a contact endstop triggers and logs the trigger height. Two scan types are shown: a grid scan of a surface, and radial scans that probe inward from start points on a hemisphere.
