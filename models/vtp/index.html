VTP foam durability tests

Measured mechanical tests of foams made by viscous thread printing (VTP): a 3D-printing variant in which the extruded thread coils to set porosity. The groups include printed PLA and TPU foams, laminates with surface coats, cyclic and long-duration TPU tests, three-point bending, and copper-filled PLA foams. Curves are downsampled for the web; peaks are preserved.
